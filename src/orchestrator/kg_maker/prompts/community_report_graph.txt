# Goal
Write a compact, evidence-grounded report for a community of PA policy entities and relationships. The report should explain how the community helps determine brand-level access parameters.

- step therapy requirements and AND/OR paths

- phototherapy/PUVA requirements

- specialist requirements
- quantity limits
- initial authorization duration

- exceptions such as contraindication, intolerance, inadequate response, or inability to use

{{
